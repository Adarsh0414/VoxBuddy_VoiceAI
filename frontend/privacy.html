VoxBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 — Phase 1/2 build. This is written for the current, real state of the app, not aspirational future features.

What we collect

  • Email or phone number — whichever you sign in with. Used only to send you a one-time login code and identify your account. We do not require both; either alone is enough to create an account.
  • Conversation history — if you complete a conversation, the transcript (what was said, in which languages) is saved to your account so you can view it later in History.
  • Basic usage stats — conversation count, languages used, and time talked, computed from your saved conversation history.

What we do not collect

  • We do not store your login code (OTP) in plain text — it's hashed, and discarded after use or expiry.
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising. There are no ads in VoxBuddy.
  • We do not require a password, and we don't store one.

How long we keep it

Your account and conversation history are kept until you delete them. You can clear your entire conversation history at any time from Settings, or by contacting us directly. Login codes expire after 5 minutes whether used or not.

Who can see your data

Your conversation history is private to your account — other users cannot see it. If you use VoxBuddy without signing in (the anonymous testing mode), conversations aren't tied to any account and are visible to anyone with backend access, which is why we recommend signing in for real use.

Third parties

When configured with real providers (not the default local/test setup), VoxBuddy uses:

  • Brevo — to deliver login codes by email or SMS. Brevo receives your email/phone number and the code, nothing else.
  • Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini) — to translate conversation text, when configured (one or the other, not both). The provider receives the text being translated and recent conversation context, not your account identity.
  • AssemblyAI — to transcribe your microphone audio into text, when configured.
  • ElevenLabs — to generate spoken audio of the translated text, when configured.

Your rights

You can delete your conversation history at any time. To delete your account entirely, contact us using the details below.
