What VoxBuddy is

VoxBuddy is a real-time speech translation app: it listens to a conversation, figures out who you're talking to versus background noise, translates what's said, and (where configured) speaks the translation back. It is not a certified interpreter, and it is not a substitute for a professional human interpreter in legal, medical, or safety-critical situations.

Accuracy — no guarantee

Translation, transcription, and speaker-identification are all produced by third-party AI systems (see Privacy Policy for which ones, when configured with real providers) and a rule-based conversation engine. All of these can and will make mistakes: mishearing words, mistranslating meaning, or misidentifying who's speaking. Do not rely on VoxBuddy for decisions where a mistranslation could cause financial, legal, medical, or physical harm.
