Help & feedback
Q
How does VoxBuddy know who I'm talking to?
VoxBuddy quietly combines a few signals — the sound of a voice, natural back-and-forth timing, and how well what's said fits the conversation so far — to work out who you're talking to. You don't select anyone manually; it updates automatically as the conversation happens.
Q
Can I talk to more than one person at once?
Yes — VoxBuddy can follow up to two conversation partners at the same time, so it works for situations like a couple at a market stall or two colleagues in a meeting. Anyone beyond that is treated as a bystander and filtered out.
Q
Is my conversation history private?
Your conversation history is tied to your account and only visible when you're signed in — VoxBuddy doesn't sell it, share it, or use it for advertising. You can review or clear it anytime from Privacy & data in Settings.
Q
How does real-time translation work?
When you speak, VoxBuddy turns your speech into text, uses its Conversation Intelligence Engine to confirm who's talking, translates that text, and speaks the translation out loud. This happens continuously as the conversation goes on.
Q
Which languages does VoxBuddy support?
Supported languages depend on the speech recognition, translation, and voice providers configured for your VoxBuddy deployment, so the exact list can vary. You pick your own spoken language during setup, and VoxBuddy auto-detects the other person's language as they talk.
Q
Do I need earbuds to use VoxBuddy?
No — earbuds are optional. Setup lets you connect Bluetooth earbuds for a more private, hands-free experience, but you can skip that step and use your device's own microphone and speaker instead.
Q
Does VoxBuddy store my voice or audio?
Your saved conversation history contains the transcribed text and its translation, not raw audio recordings. Your voice is sent to the configured speech-recognition and speech-synthesis services to produce that text and spoken output, but audio itself isn't kept as part of your account's history.
Q
Can I delete my conversation history?
Yes. Go to Settings > Privacy & data and tap "Clear my conversation history" — this permanently deletes your saved conversations and resets your stats. Your account itself isn't affected, so you can keep using VoxBuddy right after.
Q
Can I delete my VoxBuddy account?
Yes. Go to Profile and tap Delete account, then confirm — VoxBuddy permanently removes your profile and every saved conversation, and signs you out everywhere. This can't be undone; signing back in afterward starts a brand-new account from scratch.
Q
What happens if a translation is incorrect?
VoxBuddy's translations are generated by AI and, like any automated translation, can occasionally get nuance, slang, or context wrong. For anything important — medical, legal, or financial details — it's worth double-checking or confirming directly with the other person.
Q
Does VoxBuddy work offline?
Not for live conversations — real-time speech recognition, translation, and speech synthesis all need an internet connection. The app itself can still open offline thanks to basic app-shell caching, but you'll need a connection to start or continue an actual conversation.
Q
What happens if my internet connection drops?
VoxBuddy shows an offline notice and automatically attempts to reconnect your conversation once your connection returns. If it can't reconnect, you may need to tap End and then Start Conversation again to begin a fresh session.
Q
Which AI/cloud services power VoxBuddy?
VoxBuddy's live pipeline uses AWS Transcribe for speech recognition, Gemini for translation, and Amazon Polly to turn translations into speech. Conversation history is stored in Amazon DynamoDB where that's enabled for your deployment.
Q
Is my conversation used to train AI models?
VoxBuddy has no feature that collects your conversations to train its own models. Your speech and text are sent to the configured speech-recognition, translation, and voice providers only to produce your conversation in real time — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what those providers receive.
Q
How does VoxBuddy protect my account?
You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email or phone, or with Google Sign-In — VoxBuddy never asks for or stores a password. Login codes expire quickly and aren't kept in plain text, and your session uses a private access token that's checked on every request.
Q
Can I use VoxBuddy for group conversations?
VoxBuddy supports small group conversations, tracking up to two active conversation partners alongside you at once. It's built for everyday situations like a couple at a stall or two colleagues in a meeting, rather than large groups with many simultaneous speakers.
Q
What is Demo Mode?
Demo Mode is a pre-login walkthrough that shows how VoxBuddy's Conversation Intelligence Engine decides who's a conversation partner versus a bystander, using preset example scenarios. It doesn't use your microphone, an account, or any live translation service — real conversations happen through Start Conversation once you're signed in.
Q
What should I do if VoxBuddy is not detecting speech?
Check that VoxBuddy has microphone permission, that the right input device (your phone's mic or connected earbuds) is selected, and that you have a stable internet connection. Also confirm you're using a supported, up-to-date browser or device. If speech still isn't detected, tap End and Start Conversation again to begin a fresh session.
Q
What should I do if I cannot hear the translated speech?
Check your device volume and that the right output — earbuds or phone speaker — is selected, and make sure your browser has audio playback permission. A patchy connection or a temporarily unavailable voice provider can also cause silence. If it still doesn't work, tap End and Start Conversation again.
